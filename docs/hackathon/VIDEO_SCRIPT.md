# HushhBoard × PayPal: demo video script (v9)

**Target length:** 2:50 (hard limit 3:00) · **Format:** 1080p, portrait phone recording centred on a 16:9 frame
**Rules:** no music · no third-party logos (plain SMS app, not WhatsApp; stop before the M-PESA app opens) · PayPal sandbox only
**How to record:** one phone screen recording per scene, then one voice recording per scene (phone voice recorder, quiet room, phone 20 cm from mouth). Send all clips; editing, captions, callouts and title cards are done after.

---

## Before you press record
- [ ] Clear HushhBoard's data (Settings → Apps → HushhBoard → Storage → Clear data) so setup starts fresh
- [ ] Open https://hushhboard-money.onrender.com once, 1 minute before (wakes the server)
- [ ] Sandbox account A ("you") ready to log in; sandbox account B ("Jane") logged in on the laptop
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on, battery above 50 %, brightness up, status bar tidy
- [ ] From another phone, have "Jane" send you by SMS: `Hey! Send me $12 on PayPal to <Jane's sandbox email> for the logo 🙏`
- [ ] A second chat (any contact) to use for the Shop scene

---

## Scene 1 · Hook · 0:00–0:14
**Screen:** title card (added in editing) → the SMS chat with Jane's message, keyboard open.
**Say:** "In Kenya, money moves in chats. *Send me two thousand. Pay for the logo. Can you get me this?* HushhBoard is a keyboard that turns those messages into payments. And now, with PayPal, they go global."
**On screen:** "HushhBoard × PayPal"

## Scene 2 · Setup · 0:14–0:36 *(sped up 2× in editing)*
**Screen:** Turn on → Choose HushhBoard → Allow → **Connect your money**: Mobile money → type your 07… number → Bank account → pick a bank → type account number → PayPal → PayPal sandbox login → back to the app.
**Say:** "Setup is a few taps. I add my M-PESA number, my bank, and PayPal. PayPal runs its own login, so HushhBoard never sees my password. It only keeps a sealed link to my account."
**On screen:** "Your password stays with PayPal"

## Scene 3 · The Wallet card · 0:36–0:50
**Screen:** Wallet. Card shows PayPal → swipe → bank → swipe → M-PESA. Tap the eye to show details, tap again to hide.
**Say:** "All my money lives on one card. Swipe to switch accounts. And the eye hides my details when someone's looking over my shoulder."

## Scene 4 · AI fill + PayPal payment · 0:50–1:32 *(the hero shot, take your time)*
**Screen:** SMS chat → long-press Jane's message → Copy → tap **Pay** on the keyboard toolbar → tap the suggestion at the top → the form fills: USD 12, Bank → PayPal, Jane's email, PayPal bar selected → **Pay with PayPal** → **Continue to PayPal** → PayPal's sheet → **Pay** → back to HushhBoard: **Paid**.
**Say:** "Jane needs twelve dollars for a logo. I copy her message and tap Pay, right on the keyboard. On-device AI reads the amount, the currency and her email, and picks PayPal. One tap fills it all in. PayPal opens its own sheet, I confirm… and it's paid. I never left the chat."
**On screen:** "On-device AI: your message never leaves the phone" · "Real PayPal Orders API (sandbox)"
**Retake tip:** copy the message again before each take (only messages copied in the last 3 minutes are offered).

## Scene 5 · Shillings ↔ dollars · 1:32–1:46
**Screen:** Pay → type **5000** (KSh) → swipe up on the amount → **USD 38.xx**, line below "≈ KSh 5,000 · today's rate" → swipe again → back to KSh.
**Say:** "Kenyans think in shillings. I type shillings, swipe, and it's dollars, at today's live rate. Swipe back, and it's shillings again."
**On screen:** "Live exchange rate"

## Scene 6 · Shop from any chat (Channel3) · 1:46–2:12
**Screen:** second chat → type `can you get me wireless earbuds` → tap the shopping suggestion (or the bag key) → the Shop sheet slides up over the chat with live prices → scroll one card → tap **Request** on a product → the PayPal link appears in the chat.
**Say:** "Shopping starts in chats too. One tap, and Channel3 finds live prices across thousands of stores, right over the conversation. I tap Request, and a PayPal pay link drops into the chat so a friend can chip in."
**On screen:** "Live prices by Channel3"
**Note:** product titles show brand names; we'll blur them in editing if needed.

## Scene 7 · Kenya's rails, honestly · 2:12–2:32
**Screen:** Pay → **Phone** tab → your own number → PayPal bar → Pay → the **Withdrawal** dialog → tap **Open M-PESA**, cut on the "Amount copied" toast (before M-PESA opens). Then: someone else's number → PayPal → the **Credit · ready, waiting for the API** hint.
**Say:** "PayPal to my own M-PESA is a withdrawal. HushhBoard hands me to the official M-PESA PayPal service with the amount ready. PayPal straight to someone else's M-PESA or bank is built, and waiting for those APIs. We only move money where the rails really exist."

## Scene 8 · Get paid · 2:32–2:46
**Screen:** Wallet → **Request** → $25 → Create link → share to the SMS chat → *(cut)* laptop: Jane opens the link and pays → *(cut)* phone: Wallet → Recent shows **Paid**, the card shows ↓ $25.
**Say:** "And freelancers can request dollars from any client, in any chat, and watch it land."

## Scene 9 · Close · 2:46–2:56
**Screen:** Wallet card → end card (added in editing).
**Say:** "M-PESA, banks and PayPal. One keyboard. Any chat. This is HushhBoard."
**On screen:** "HushhBoard · Built with PayPal, Channel3 and Render · github.com/BrianKachumba/HushhBoard_PayPal"
